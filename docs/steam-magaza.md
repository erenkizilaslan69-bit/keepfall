## 1. Kısa açıklama (≤ 300 karakter)

- **EN (255):** Build a castle, turn workers into soldiers and bluff your rivals. Every player secretly rules one of four kingdoms; fog hides armies, envoys carry lies, and one battle at the gate can end a castle. Short pixel-art strategy for 2–4 players or against bots.
- **TR (245):** Kaleni kur, işçileri askere çevir, rakiplerini kandır. Herkes gizlice dört krallıktan birini yönetir; sis orduları saklar, ulaklar yalan taşır, kapıdaki tek savaş bir kaleyi düşürür. 2–4 kişilik ya da botlara karşı kısa, piksel sanatlı strateji.

### EN

```
[h2]Every soldier is a worker lost.[/h2]

Keepfall is a short castle strategy game in pixel art. You have one castle, a handful of workers and one dilemma: a worker keeps the economy running, a soldier does not. The bigger your army, the less food, wood and iron you have. Matches last about 15 minutes, so every choice counts.

[h2]Who deceived whom?[/h2]

Each player secretly rules one of [b]four kingdoms[/b], and nobody knows which. The Shadow Kingdom marches phantom armies and writes false scout reports. The Merchant Republic bribes marching armies and hires bandit camps. The Seers see every kingdom and spot the tricks. The Schemers send armies and envoys under someone else's name. When the match ends, the game tells you [b]who deceived whom[/b], minute by minute.

[h2]Fog, scouts, envoys, bluffs[/h2]

You only see your own castle and what the fog has opened. A scout tells you roughly what a rival has, and it can be wrong. An envoy carries goods and a short message to another player, and it never has to be true. You can recall or redirect an army on the road. Read the fog, read the people.

[h2]One gate, one battle, up to three armies[/h2]

Armies march along roads, meet on them, and fight at the castle gate. Win at the gate and the castle falls in a single blow; half of the defending army is taken prisoner. A second army that arrives while a siege is on does not wait: it joins the fight, and three sides can clash at one gate. Only the last army standing takes the castle.

[h2]Seven units, pick four[/h2]

Swordsman, Spearman, Shield-bearer, Archer, Monk, Catapult and Cavalry. You pick your deck before the match and every unit has a counter. Cavalry cannot take a castle alone, but it burns the fields. After the preparation (5 minutes) comes the war (10 minutes) and, later, the famine.

[h2]Challenges and bots[/h2]

Play alone against Easy, Medium or Hard bots, learn the game in handmade Challenges that each teach one idea, and try the daily challenge that is the same for everyone. 30 achievements and mastery levels for every kingdom unlock banners and avatar frames: they are only for show and never change the balance. [MP] Friends join with a room code.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] Short matches: 5 minutes of preparation + 10 minutes of war
[*] One gate, one battle: a castle falls in a single blow, up to three armies at one gate
[*] Four secret kingdoms, each with its own kind of deception
[*] Fog of war, scouts, envoys, phantom armies, bribes and disguises
[*] A "who deceived whom?" recap at the end of every match
[*] Seven units, a four-unit deck and a 7×7 counter table
[*] Bots (Easy, Medium, Hard), handmade Challenges and a daily challenge
[*] [MP] 2–4 players, private rooms with a room code
[*] 30 achievements, kingdom mastery, cosmetic-only unlocks
[*] English and Turkish
[/list]

Art: Tiny Swords by Pixel Frog.
```

1. Steamworks'te app id alınır (hesap onayı, 100 USD ücret: Eren).
2. `[MP]` kararına göre metin/etiketler düzeltilir; kısa açıklama ≤ 300 karakter (EN 255, TR 245: tamam).
3. Tüm grafikler yüklenir (§9, seçilen seçenek); ekran görüntüleri 5–8 adet sırayla (§8).
4. Yapay zekâ beyanı ve içerik anketi doldurulur (§5, §6); yaş derecelendirmesi anketten çıkar.
5. Sistem gereksinimleri (D3D11 dahil) girilir (§3). Sayfada geçmemesi gerekenler: mobil, Steam Deck/Linux/macOS, kontrolcü, mod desteği, sesli sohbet, bulut kayıt, Steam başarımları (SDK entegrasyonu denenmedi).
6. "Coming Soon" olarak yayımlanır; sayfa gözden geçirmesi (Valve) ~birkaç gün sürebilir.
