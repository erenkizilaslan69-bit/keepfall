# Source of the swear-word lists

The lists in this folder (`de, es, fr, it, pt, pl, ru, zh, ja, ko`, and the extra English words) are a **curated subset** of
"List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words" by Shutterstock / the LDNOOBW project,
https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words ,
licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .

What was changed: only real swear words and slurs were kept (the full lists also hold ordinary words and sexual vocabulary); entries that are everyday words in another
language were dropped; word forms were added as `stem*` entries. `uk.txt` (Ukrainian) is hand-written: that project has no Ukrainian list.
The Turkish and English core lists live in `EnvoyMessage.cs`.
